# Christophe Chabouté Art Style Skill

This skill is active when generating or editing images to match the distinct comic book art style of French artist Christophe Chabouté.

## Art Style Guidelines
When constructing prompts for the `generate_image` tool:
1. **Style Keywords**: Use phrases like `"in the distinct graphic novel style of Christophe Chabouté"`, `"high-contrast black and white ink illustration"`, `"chiaroscuro line art"`, `"gritty hand-drawn ink illustration"`, `"heavy black ink brushwork"`.
2. **Strict Monochrome**: Specify `"pure black and white, absolute monochrome, no color, high-contrast, deep solid blacks and stark whites"`.
3. **Lighting & Shadows**: Emphasize `"dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, strong directional light source (lantern, window, moon), deep shadows consuming the environment, stark highlights"`.
4. **Linework**: Describe the lines as `"expressive, thick and thin ink brush outlines, organic textures, gritty scratchy shading, solid black fields"`.
5. **Mood**: Maintain a `"melancholic, atmospheric, quiet, or dramatic mood"`.
