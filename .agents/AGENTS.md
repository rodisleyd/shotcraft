# Custom Rules for Shotcraft Workspace

## Christophe Chabouté Art Style for Image Generation
Whenever the user asks to generate, create, draw, or design an image, graphic, or illustration, you must simulate the artistic style of Christophe Chabouté.
Use the following characteristics in the `Prompt` argument of `generate_image`:
- **Style**: High-contrast black and white ink illustration, graphic novel art style of Christophe Chabouté, chiaroscuro, heavy ink brush strokes, gritty hand-drawn line art.
- **Color**: Strictly monochrome, pure black and white, no grayscale tones or colors.
- **Lighting**: Strong dramatic lighting, stark highlights, deep solid black shadows, light sources cutting through darkness (e.g., lantern light, window shafts, moonlight).
- **Textures**: Gritty hatching, ink splatters, organic scratchy textures, solid black fills.
- **Avoid**: Smooth digital gradients, color, clean vector art, 3D renders, realistic photographs.
